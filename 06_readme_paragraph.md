# README-ready finding (justified)

**8. (Q new) Recent progress reached the whole herd for production and type, but the
functional domains declined across their full distribution, and animals are becoming more
specialized.**

Tracking distribution percentiles by birth cohort, LPI improved at every level, with the
weakest tenth rising faster than the top (P10 +83.5/yr vs P90 +58.1/yr): the gains reached
the whole herd, not just the best animals. Reproduction and environmental efficiency moved
the opposite way, declining at P10, P50 and P90 alike, so their weakness is
distribution-wide, not an artefact of the average; environmental efficiency has the deepest
weak tail (P10 = 384). No single subindex shows a variance change surviving FDR correction,
but cross-subindex dispersion, how far apart each animal's six domains are, is rising
robustly (+3.6/yr, sire-clustered bootstrap CI [+2.08, +4.99]): recent animals are more
specialized, and a high LPI can coexist with a weak individual domain. This is a mixed
result: broad, healthy progress on the selected axis alongside a distribution-wide decline
in unselected functional domains and increasing specialization.
