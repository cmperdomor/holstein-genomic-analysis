# Notebook 06 - Producer Summary (plain English)

**The question:** does this herd still have room to select, and did recent progress lift
every animal or just the average?

**What the data shows:**

Your production-and-type breeding is working across the board. LPI rose in every part of
the herd, and the weakest animals improved *faster* than the best ones, so the bottom is
catching up, not being left behind. Type has the most useful selection room: it is both
wide and strong, so you have good animals to choose between.

Reproduction and environmental efficiency went the other way. They fell at every level,
top, middle and bottom, so this is not just a low average hiding good animals. The weakest
tenth of the herd for environmental efficiency now sits very low (P10 around 384). Health
is roughly holding.

Your animals are also becoming **more specialized**: the gap between each cow's strongest
and weakest domain is widening year on year. Recent cows are less "balanced across the
board" and more "great at some things, weak at others." A high LPI cow can still be poor
in one domain, so top overall merit does not guarantee an all-round animal.

**What this means for decisions:**

- Keep pushing production and type; the whole distribution is improving.
- Watch the reproduction and environment lower tails; they are your persistent weak spots.
- Before adding any minimum reproduction or environment threshold to sire selection, run
  it through a selection-index simulation first, do not set it from these numbers alone.
- If you value all-round cows, the rising specialization is worth monitoring; it is not
  automatically bad, but it is a real change.
